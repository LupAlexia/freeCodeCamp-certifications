Nikola Tesla

The man who pulled light from the void and gave it form

Tesla's most remarkable life-long achievements:

  • 1882 - Discovered the rotating magnetic field, a fundamental principle in AC power.
  • 1883 - Built a working prototype of an induction motor in Strasbourg.
  • 1887 - Invented and demonstrated the Tesla coil, used in radio tech and wireless power.
  • 1895 - Built one of the first hydroelectric power plants at Niagara Falls, operational by 1896.
  • 1898 - Demonstrated a radio-controlled boat, one of the earliest uses of wireless remote control.
  • 1901 - Constructed the Wardenclyffe Tower for wireless transmission of energy and communication.
“Tesla was one of the most brilliant and tragic figures in the history of science. A man ahead of his time.” — Albert Einstein
